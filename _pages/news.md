## News & Announcements

**September 2026**
New open access paper in *Polymer Composites*: ["Lichen-Derived Mineral Residue as a Renewable Particulate Filler in Polylactic Acid Biocomposites"](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/pc.71612), with Necip Elbir, Mine Begüm Alanalp and Ali Durmuş. The study introduces höllük, a lichen residue traditionally used in southeastern Turkey, as an untreated filler for PLA.
